CASE 02

Taxi Service — Google Search Ads (New Zealand)

Campaign typeGoogle Search Ads
Daily budgetNZ$11.75/day (~₹600/day)
Min. booking valueNZ$50
LocationNew Zealand
MetricMayJuneImprovement
Clicks278252-9.4%
Conversions2532+28%
Ad spendNZ$363NZ$367Nearly same
Cost per conversionNZ$14.55NZ$11.47-22%

Although total clicks decreased by 9.4%, campaign optimisation significantly improved lead quality, resulting in a 28% increase in conversions while maintaining nearly the same advertising budget. Cost per conversion decreased by 22% — and with a minimum booking value of NZ$50, acquiring leads at roughly NZ$11.47 each created a strong opportunity for profitable customer acquisition.

Optimisation Strategy
  1. 1Keyword bid & budget analysis — researched keywords via Google Keyword Planner, compared Top of Page bidding ranges, and selected terms whose estimated CPC aligned with the NZ$11.75 daily budget.
  2. 2Search intent optimisation — focused spend on high-intent keywords like "Airport Taxi," "Taxi Near Me," and "Book Taxi Online" to improve traffic quality.
  3. 3Search terms & negative keywords — reviewed search term reports weekly and added irrelevant queries as negatives to cut wasted spend.
  4. 4Bid optimisation — raised bids on strong-converting keywords and reduced them on expensive, low-converting terms.
  5. 5Ad copy optimisation — tested variations highlighting 24/7 availability, airport transfers, fixed pricing, and fast pickup.
  6. 6Location & time-based optimisation — identified top-performing areas and scheduled ads around peak booking hours.
  7. 7Conversion tracking & monitoring — reviewed performance weekly at the keyword level and reallocated budget toward the lowest cost-per-conversion terms.
Outcome
✓ 28% increase in leads — 32 qualified leads in June vs. 25 in May.
✓ Lower cost per lead — cost per conversion down from NZ$14.55 to NZ$11.47.
✓ Improved budget efficiency — more conversions on almost the same monthly spend.
✓ Higher-quality traffic — refined targeting brought in users more likely to book.

This campaign shows how data-driven optimisation — keyword selection, bid management, and continuous performance analysis — can lift lead generation without increasing spend. By focusing on user intent and budget efficiency, it delivered more conversions at a lower acquisition cost.
